# Part II: Data Engineering

Models eat data, and somebody has to cook. This part turns you into that somebody: SQL, modeling, formats, distributed processing, orchestration, streaming, and keeping the whole thing secure and observable.

About 230 hours in total.

| Module | Hours | What it's about |
|---|---|---|
| [08. SQL and Relational Databases](08-sql-relational/) | 45 | SQL is fifty years old and it will outlive most of the frameworks you are excited about today. |
| [09. Data Modeling](09-data-modeling/) | 20 | The schema is the API of your data. Design it badly and every downstream team pays interest forever. |
| [10. Data Wrangling and Analysis](10-data-wrangling/) | 40 | Eighty percent of the job, zero percent of the glamour. |
| [11. Storage, Formats and Architecture](11-storage-formats/) | 20 | Where the bytes live, what shape they have, and why that decides your cloud bill. |
| [12. Distributed Processing](12-distributed-processing/) | 25 | When one machine is not enough, and how to tell whether it really isn't. |
| [13. Pipelines and Orchestration](13-pipelines-orchestration/) | 30 | A pipeline that only works when you run it by hand is a hobby, not a pipeline. |
| [14. Streaming](14-streaming/) | 25 | Data that doesn't wait for your nightly batch job. |
| [15. Governance, Security and Reliability](15-governance-security/) | 25 | Your data platform is the most valuable target in the company. Treat it that way. |
